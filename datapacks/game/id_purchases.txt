to add new contraptions, you'll need to alter:
1. hardcode:cont.purchase.unlocked_scoreboards.mcfunction - creating a scoreboard that stores whether the player is able to purchase the contraption
2. hardcode:cont.purchase.unlocking.mcfunction - to display a message to the player upon the unlocking of the contraption
3. hardcode:cont.purchase.main.mcfunction - to process what to do after a player has hit the purchase button in the menu
4. hardcode:cont.new.mcfunction - how a contraption should be formed eg summoning a falling_block entity for a cactus
5. hardcode:cont.effects_upon_removal.mcfunction - what to do to the contraption when it is removed
6. game:purchase_menu.json - to add the contraption into the purchase menu
7. game:cont_upgrades.mcfunction - what to do when the player has hit the upgrade button in the menu
8. hardcode:cont.upgrades.mcfunction - stores data about what the next upgrade would be like and the cost
9. hardcode:generic.mob_cont_behavior.mcfunction - how the functionality of the contraption would be, apparently

and adding scoreboard players set #global game.saves.cont_unlocked.cactus 1 to whenever you'd like the contraption to be unlocked (e.g. after surviving a level)
